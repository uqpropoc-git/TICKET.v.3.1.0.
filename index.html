Cyber Security Incident Response

Alur standar: Detect → Validate → Contain → Escalate → Investigate → Recover → Document.
01

Detect & Validate

Identifikasi alert, anomalous traffic, malware, account compromise atau akses tidak sah.

02

Contain & Escalate

Isolasi endpoint/segment jaringan bila perlu, eskalasi ke IT Manager & Security Lead, simpan bukti.

03

Investigate & Recover

Root cause analysis, eradication, recovery terverifikasi, dan laporan lesson learned.
